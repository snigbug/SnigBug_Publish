# Notion Press — Self-Publishing Platform Requirements

Base URL: https://notionpress.com/
Source: public site content (homepage, /publish, /pricing) captured 2026-09-11.

## Publishing Workflow

- The platform advertises a 3-step self-publishing workflow: Design Your Book → Publish in both formats → Distribute Globally
- "Design Your Book" lets an author design the cover and interior using built-in design tools and templates
- Authors can release a title simultaneously as paperback and eBook
- Published titles are distributed to 30,000+ retail stores across 150+ countries
- Multi-language publishing is supported: English, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, Malayalam, Gujarati, Kannada

## Formats & ISBN

- Supported formats: Paperback, Hardbound, eBook
- Each format (paperback, hardbound, eBook) is issued its own separate ISBN
- Printing uses a print-on-demand (POD) model driven by sales velocity, to avoid stockouts

## Royalties & Payouts

- Standard package royalty: 80% of net profit through the Notion Press Store (after a 20% distribution fee); 80% of net profit through Amazon/Flipkart/other retailers (after a 50% distribution deduction)
- Outpublish program royalty: 100% of net profit across all sales channels
- Net earnings are calculated as MRP minus production and distribution costs
- India-based online sales are paid out monthly, within 30 days
- International sales and eBook sales settle on a 90-day reconciliation cycle

## Distribution

- Sales channels include Amazon, Flipkart, and the Notion Press Store
- Total reach: 30,000+ stores across 150+ countries

## Author Tools

- The platform reports a community of 30,000+ published authors
